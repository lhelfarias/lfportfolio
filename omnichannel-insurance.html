Itaú Bank

Omnichannel Insurance

  • Service Design
  • User Research
  • UI Design
Role UX/UI Designer
Duration 1 year
Team PO, DM, Devs, QA + business and insurance-company stakeholders

Itaú Bank was mid-digital transformation. The Insurance cell — profitable but inefficient — was generating claims around resolution times, with lengthy manual processes consuming executive time that belonged elsewhere.

I designed Itaú's entire digital insurance purchase and inquiry process. The old process was entirely manual: sign the paperwork, have it picked up, send it to the insurer, wait for approval, then activate the policy on the client's page — a full week end to end. In QA, we measured the new digital flow for a life insurance policy, from registration to appearing on the client's page, at 7 minutes. A months-long comparison of digital vs. physical contracting showed a 63% reduction in operational load, and the project was recognized as one of the bank's 3 most impactful solutions that year. I proposed many changes that legal regulations often couldn't accommodate — the final call belonged to Legal and the client (the insurance company).

Achievements: The redesign reduced the sales process from 5 working days to 7 minutes, cut operational load by 63%, and ranked among the top 3 most impactful projects at the bank that year.

Challenge: Connecting to multiple providers — each with different technical contexts and integration requirements — created significant feasibility constraints throughout the project.
