Chemical Program

  • Data Visualization
  • User Research
  • Information Architecture
Role Key Designer
Duration 6 months
Team PO, Devs, BA, QA + SLB business stakeholders

A global energy company managing chemical programs across oil wells — both remote and manual — needed to consolidate the software supporting implementation, administration, and field supervision into a single platform.

I designed the platform's 4 main sections — including a Landing that didn't exist before, giving an overview of all wells and their production status — and to prioritize what to show in each one, I interviewed clients and well operators. The previous software organized information by area of interest; I reorganized the navigation to be well-centric, grouping each well's production volume, lab analysis, and chemicals together, which is how users preferred to work. I also designed a simple modal for remotely raising or lowering chemical injection — a simple interaction for a physically delicate operation. The engagement covered research and design through handoff to development; no production data is available.

Achievements: Designed a unified monitoring experience for chemical program management across both traditional and remotely controlled wells — consolidating manual and sensor-based indicators into a single, coherent interface.

Challenge: Managing information complexity across multiple user types — each with different needs within the same implementation and monitoring flow — while keeping the interface coherent and the data actionable for all of them.
